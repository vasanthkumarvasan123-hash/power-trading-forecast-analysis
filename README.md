# Power Trading Forecast Analysis

## IEX Day-Ahead Market | 360DigiTMG Industrial Project

### Project Overview

This project analyzes electricity trading patterns in the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) Day-Ahead Market and studies the impact of weather and renewable energy on electricity prices.

### What I Did

* Analyzed **1,12,128 electricity trading records** from **January 2023 to March 2026**
* Integrated electricity market data with weather data collected from **14 locations across India**
* Performed data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL analysis, and dashboard development
* Built interactive dashboards using **Power BI** and **Excel**
* Generated business insights related to MCP trends, renewable energy impact, and market behavior

### Tools & Technologies

* Python
* MySQL
* Power BI
* Excel

### Key Findings

* MCP (Market Clearing Price) declined by **27.8%** over three years due to increased renewable energy participation
* Identified a clear **Duck Curve** pattern:

  * Hour 14 recorded the lowest average MCP (₹2,533)
  * Hour 20 recorded the highest average MCP (₹7,626)
* Market conditions shifted from **demand-short in 2023** to **supply-long in 2025**
* Solar radiation showed the strongest negative correlation with MCP (**-0.394**)

### Business Value

The project demonstrates how renewable energy growth influences electricity prices and market dynamics, helping stakeholders understand pricing behavior and energy market trends.
